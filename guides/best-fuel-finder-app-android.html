Android fuel app guide

What makes the best fuel finder app for Android?

A useful fuel price app should do more than show a list of forecourts. It should help you decide where to stop, how much you will save and whether the information is current enough to trust.

Updated 16 August 2026 · Written by the Fuel Finder UK team

1. Live prices from a reliable source

Look for a service that explains where its prices come from and how often they update. Official UK Government CMA fuel data gives drivers a consistent source for comparing participating retailers, while timestamps help you judge how current a result is.

2. Broad UK station coverage

A good app should cover more than one supermarket chain. Fuel Finder UK currently compares prices across 8,100+ participating UK stations, including major retailers and independent forecourts in the CMA scheme.

3. Price and distance in the same view

The cheapest price is not always the cheapest stop once you include a detour. Check that the app lets you sort by price or distance and open directions without having to search each retailer separately.

4. Realistic fill-cost estimates

Pence per litre does not tell the whole story. A useful Android fuel finder can estimate what a quarter, half or full tank will cost for your car, helping you compare the saving you will actually receive.

5. Features that help on real journeys

  • Petrol and diesel filters, including supported premium grades.
  • Brand filters for loyalty schemes and preferred retailers.
  • Route planning for longer journeys.
  • Station details and recent community reports.
  • Clear pricing with no account required for basic searches.

Fuel Finder UK has now passed 50,000 Android downloads, giving it a growing base of drivers using the service to compare UK fuel prices.

Our practical recommendation: if you want a free Android fuel finder, test whether it gives you current prices, useful coverage and a sensible route—not just the lowest headline number.
