Overview

Fuel Finder UK - Live Prices ("the App", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and who it is shared with. The App does not require an account, sign-up, or login.

Most of your information — your saved vehicles, favourites, preferences, and searches — stays on your device. Some features, described below, require data to be sent to UK government services or to our own and third-party servers in order to work. This policy explains exactly when that happens.

Information We Handle

1. Location Data

  • What: Approximate and precise location from your device, and any start/destination you enter for route planning.
  • Why: To show fuel stations near you, sort them by distance, and (in Plan Route) calculate a driving route and find the cheapest fuel along it.
  • Where it goes: Sorting stations by distance happens on your device. When you use Plan Route, the start and destination coordinates are sent over HTTPS through Cloudflare to our own routing server. Routes are calculated using the open-source Valhalla routing engine and OpenStreetMap road data; your journey is not sent to Google, Apple, an advertising provider, or another third-party routing provider. Map tiles and ads (see below) may allow their providers to infer your approximate location from your IP address.
  • Storage: A computed route may be cached for up to seven days, keyed only by its rounded start and destination coordinates, so repeat requests can be served more quickly. It is not linked to you, a device identifier, or an account. Your IP address is processed transiently for abuse prevention and rate-limiting and is not stored with the route. We do not create a location history or movement profile.

2. Vehicle Information (My Cars)

  • What: The vehicle registration number you choose to enter, and the details returned for it (make, model, fuel type, MOT history, tax and ULEZ status).
  • Why: To show your vehicle's MOT history, tax and ULEZ status, road tax rate, and to calculate fill costs based on tank size.
  • Where it goes: When you add or refresh a vehicle, the registration number is sent to the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service and the DVSA MOT History service (both UK government services) to retrieve the vehicle's details. If our bundled performance catalogue cannot identify the vehicle, its make, model, year, fuel type, engine size and CO₂ figure may be sent to our Cloudflare backend so we can return or add reviewed performance data. The registration number is never included in that request or stored on our servers.
  • Storage: Returned vehicle details and downloaded performance data are stored locally on your device. Plate-free missing-vehicle attributes may be retained on our backend to improve the performance catalogue.

3. Community Reports

  • What: Reports you choose to submit (for example: out-of-fuel, queue times, closed station, suspected price errors), the station the report relates to, the report time, and your votes on other reports.
  • Why: To show real-time station status to other drivers and let the community verify reports.
  • Where it goes: Reports and votes are sent to and stored in our cloud database, hosted on Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, and are visible to other users of the App. Stored report fields are the station identifier, report type, fuel type (where relevant), queue estimate (where relevant), vote direction, and timestamps. Reports are not linked to a name, account, device identifier, or advertising identifier.
  • Storage: Each report is set to expire after six hours. Google may process standard connection metadata, such as an IP address, while delivering the Firestore service, but we do not store it in the report.
  • Rate limiting: Anti-spam limits for Community Reports are maintained locally on your device.

4. User Preferences

  • What: Selected fuel type and grade, preferred brands, search radius, sort order, theme and accent colour, and favourite stations.
  • Storage: Stored locally on your device only and never sent to our servers.

5. Advertising & Identifiers

  • What: Subject to your platform and consent choices, your device's advertising identifier (Apple IDFA or Android Advertising ID), device information, and ad-interaction data may be used to serve, measure, and personalise ads. Where consent is not given or personalised advertising is unavailable, Google may serve limited or non-personalised ads in accordance with your choices and applicable law.
  • How we ask: On iPhone and iPad we use Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before allowing tracking. Where required, including in the UK/EEA, we use Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and manage advertising consent choices.
  • Provider: Google AdMob (see Third-Party Services).
  • Rewarded routes (iOS only): On iPhone and iPad, after the monthly free road-route allowance is used, free users may optionally view a rewarded advertisement to unlock the specific road route they have requested. The route is calculated before the ad is offered, and is displayed only after AdMob confirms completion. Declining or skipping the ad does not prevent use of the free straight-line route. Up to three rewarded road routes may be used per day. Rewarded route advertisements are not currently offered in the Android app.

6. Device & Diagnostic Information

  • What: Basic technical information (such as iOS or Android version, device type and screen characteristics), diagnostic information made available by the platform or service providers, and your IP address, which is necessarily visible to any server the App contacts.
  • Why: To deliver content, ensure compatibility, and keep the service secure and reliable.

How We Use Your Information

  1. Find and rank nearby fuel stations based on your location.
  2. Display fuel prices sourced from official UK government (CMA) open data.
  3. Plan routes and find the cheapest fuel along your journey.
  4. Show your vehicle's MOT, tax and ULEZ information in My Cars.
  5. Power Community Reports so drivers can share live station status.
  6. Remember your preferences and improve app performance.
  7. Show advertising (unless you have purchased the ad-free upgrade).

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • ❌ Sell your personal information.
  • ❌ Share your precise location or journey with advertisers.
  • ❌ Send your journey to a third-party routing provider.
  • ❌ Create a profile of your movements or location history.
  • ❌ Store your vehicle registration number on our own servers.
  • ❌ Require an account, name, or email address to use the App.

Data Retention

  • On-device data (preferences, favourites, saved vehicles and their details) stays on your device and is deleted when you remove it in the App or uninstall.
  • Community Reports are set to expire after six hours and may be removed sooner. Because they are not linked to an account, contact us if you need a specific report removed.
  • Computed routes may be cached for up to seven days using rounded start and destination coordinates. They are not linked to a user, device or account.
  • Vehicle registration numbers are sent to DVLA/DVSA only to perform a lookup and are not stored on our servers.
  • Missing-vehicle attributes (make, model, year, fuel type, engine size and CO₂) may be retained without a registration number so we can prioritise and publish missing performance data.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the data and services involved. App traffic to our backend, including route requests, is protected in transit using HTTPS. Nearby-station location processing and preferences remain on your device except where this policy explains that a feature requires transmission. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Rights and Choices

Location Permissions

You can revoke location access at any time. On iPhone or iPad, use Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Fuel Finder UK. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Fuel Finder UK → Permissions → Location (wording may vary by device). You can still search by entering a location manually if you deny access.

Tracking & Ad Personalisation

Use Privacy Choices inside the App where that option is available to review advertising consent. On iPhone or iPad, you can also change tracking permission via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and control Apple personalised ads via Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising. On Android, you can manage or reset the Advertising ID and ad privacy choices in Google settings (the exact path varies by Android version). Purchasing the one-time ad-free upgrade removes all ads, including rewarded ads where offered.

Data Deletion

  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all data stored on your device.
  • To request removal of a Community Report, email support@fuelfinderuk.live.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. It contains no age-inappropriate content.

Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

For users in the UK and EU, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: Location access, App Tracking Transparency, and personalised advertising.
  • Legitimate Interest: Providing nearby station results, Community Reports, security, and app reliability.
  • Performance of a service you request: Vehicle (DVLA/DVSA) lookups and route calculation, which run only when you ask for them.

Your GDPR Rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because the App holds most data only on your device and uses no accounts, many of these are exercised directly on your device. For anything else, contact support@fuelfinderuk.live. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Data Sources

  • UK Government Open Data Scheme: Fuel prices published by UK retailers under the Motor Fuel Price (Open Data) Regulations 2025, administered by the Competition and Markets Authority (CMA). Updated approximately every 30 minutes.
